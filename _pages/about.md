**Dr. Humberto Bernal** is an Economics Researcher and University Professor (Ph.D. in Economics, Universidad de los Andes, 2015) with over 20 years of academic, multilateral, and public-sector experience. His research bridges **Dynamic Mechanism Design, Applied Microeconomics, Game Theory, and Empirical Industrial Organization**, leveraging **Artificial Intelligence and agentic frameworks** to solve complex market failures. His core contributions encompass algorithmic labor market matching, rational type discovery under asymmetric information, incentive-compatible auctions for public concessions, and indirect mechanism design (currently under international peer review). His scholarship is extensively published in internationally indexed peer-reviewed journals in economic regulation, market structure, and public policy.
